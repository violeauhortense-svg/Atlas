# Financial Objectives

## Primary objective

Target: >= €3,000/month personal income within approximately 6 months.

## Decision framework

Prioritize opportunities according to:
- speed to first sale;
- customer pain;
- willingness to pay;
- gross margin;
- recurring revenue potential;
- organic acquisition potential;
- automation potential;
- maintenance burden.

Agents must not assume that launching more products is always better. Capital and attention should follow traction.

## Required reporting

Track at minimum:
- revenue;
- MRR where applicable;
- number of paying customers;
- conversion rate;
- acquisition source;
- content performance;
- refund rate;
- support load;
- product-level profitability.
