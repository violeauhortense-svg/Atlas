# Atlas Mission

Atlas exists to create and operate digital products through a human-led, AI-native venture studio.

The human board owns vision, strategy, ethics, money and irreversible external actions.

AI agents own research, execution, documentation, production and analysis within their assigned authority.

The near-term objective is to build a profitable first product and work toward €3,000/month of personal income within approximately six months.

The long-term objective is a repeatable studio capable of launching multiple products without rebuilding its operating system each time.
