# Product Launch Process

1. Capture idea.
2. Research market.
3. Decide GO / TEST / STOP.
4. Define MVP.
5. Define pricing.
6. Create lightweight brand.
7. Build MVP.
8. QA.
9. Prepare domain/email/payment/social accounts.
10. Human approval gate.
11. Launch.
12. Publish content.
13. Measure revenue and conversion.
14. Decide accelerate / optimize / test / stop.
15. Store learnings in Atlas knowledge base.

For social media, use a staged approach:
- MVP: only the highest-value channel(s);
- validated traction: expand to additional relevant channels;
- growth: operate a full product-specific ecosystem.

Each validated product should have isolated brand, email and social identities.
