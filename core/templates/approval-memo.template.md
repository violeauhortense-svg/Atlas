## Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: Market Too Small
- **Likelihood:** Low
- **Impact:** Critical (kills the product)
- **Mitigation:** We've validated $2M TAM through interviews; if SAM < $500K by week 2, we pivot or kill

### Risk 2: Strong Competition
- **Likelihood:** Medium
- **Impact:** Medium (reduces addressable market)
- **Mitigation:** Our AI differentiation is hard to replicate; focus on niche (executives, not general email)

### Risk 3: Technical Feasibility
- **Likelihood:** Low
- **Impact:** High (delays launch)
- **Mitigation:** MVP is achievable in 2 weeks (validated by fullstack dev); architecture is straightforward

### Risk 4: Customer Acquisition Expensive
- **Likelihood:** Medium
- **Impact:** Medium (affects profitability timeline)
- **Mitigation:** Organic marketing (content) first; ads only if organic CAC > $50

- Market validation: $2M TAM, 10+ customer interviews confirm demand
- Differentiation: AI-powered email assistant is not saturated (3 direct competitors, but each weak in one area)
- Achievable: MVP in 2 weeks, profitability realistic in 6 weeks
- Aligned: Fits Atlas portfolio strategy (B2B SaaS, recurring revenue, high margin)

### 🔄 PIVOT
**Market viable but positioning needs work. Adjust and retry.**

**Recommendation:**
