# Atlas Governance

Human approval is required for:
- committing meaningful financial spend;
- opening or purchasing paid services;
- publishing legally binding terms;
- changing pricing materially;
- launching a product publicly;
- creating or modifying external accounts when platform verification is required;
- sending sensitive customer communications;
- handling credentials and secrets.

Agents may prepare actions, drafts, checklists and configurations, but must stop at approval gates.

Never store secrets in Git.
Prefer Paperclip secret references or environment variables.
